# Ja-Nice Takeout image guide
Replace files in `public/images/` with authentic Ja-Nice photos using the exact filenames.
- hero.jpg — strongest food photo, ideally a full Jamaican meal
- oxtail.jpg — oxtail, jerk, curry goat or another signature plate
- shop.jpg — interior / counter
- owners.jpg — owners/staff/community photo
- food.jpg — another food spread
- storefront.jpg — exterior
The site crops images automatically.
